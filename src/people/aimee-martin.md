Hi, I'm Aimee Martin, 22 years old, from Coagh in Co. Tyrone, and I'm currently undertaking a PhD focused on developing a broadly reactive vaccine against swine influenza A virus. My research involves influenza genomics, antigen optimisation, and evaluating cross-protective immune responses against diverse viral lineages. I'm passionate about improving animal health and contributing to innovative approaches for controlling infectious diseases in livestock.
