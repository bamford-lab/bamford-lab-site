Hi, my name is Patrick Cunningham. I am a local Queen's student, living in Northern Ireland, and I completed both my biochemistry undergraduate degree and my MSci here. I am currently in the first year of my PhD, working to improve our understanding of cyanophage and cyanobacteria interactions and how we sample them, with the aim of using them as a treatment for the seasonal harmful algal blooms affecting Lough Neagh.
