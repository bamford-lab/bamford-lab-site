Hi, I'm Jiaying Gu, a PhD student in the OneZoo Centre for Doctoral Training at Queen's University Belfast, based in the Bamford AVI Lab. I studied Health Inspection and Quarantine at Guangdong Medical University before working for four years in the Microbiology Laboratory at the Shenzhen Yantian District Center for Disease Control and Prevention. In 2023, I completed a Master of Public Health at the University of Aberdeen. My PhD research examines how environmental chemical mixtures may alter immune responses and infectious disease risk in mammals. As part of a Cardiff University otter project, I investigate the viruses carried by wild otters and explore how pollution may influence host health and emerging zoonotic risks.
