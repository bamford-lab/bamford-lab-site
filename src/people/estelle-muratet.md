Hi, I'm Estelle! I'm French and lived internationally before moving to Aberdeen, Scotland to pursue my BSc in Biology. Following this, I moved to Belfast and obtained my MSc in One Health Parasitology and Infection Biology. My PhD focuses on coinfections and the interactions between viruses and microbes at a molecular and cellular level, with particular emphasis on influenza in wild birds and livestock.
