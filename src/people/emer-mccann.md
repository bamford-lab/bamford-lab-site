      My current research focuses on the surveillance of avian influenza in freshwater environments frequented by wild migratory waterbirds. Through a longitudinal study, we will collect and analyse water samples from sites across the island of Ireland to detect RNA from avian influenza viruses.

      This approach aims to provide a less invasive alternative to traditional surveillance methods, supporting earlier detection and monitoring of viral circulation, evolution and potential changes in pathogenicity.

I joined the Bamford Lab in November 2026 as a Research Fellow working on the EnviroFlu project. I recently completed my PhD at Queen's University Belfast, focusing on the environmental surveillance of livestock parasites using molecular techniques, including ddPCR and Illumina sequencing. I now bring that environmental surveillance approach to avian influenza, sampling the freshwater sites that wild migratory waterbirds use.
